# Weather Planning

The hero shows the owner's requested rescheduling notice as plain text beneath
the search panel. The former seasonal advisory widget, forecast link, and change
listeners have been removed.

The notice explains that the team will contact visitors to arrange alternative
booking dates if TMA forecasts rain or severe weather affecting their trip.
It does not connect to a weather API or automatically change bookings. The site's
existing brief flow remains a draft, not a booking service.
